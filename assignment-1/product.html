Our Products

OK-M

The Ducky OK-M is an affordable, entry-level mechanical keyboard designed for everyday use, it features hot-swappable switches, gasket-mount design and five layers of acoustic dampening for a quieter typing experience, and tri-mode connectivity. With VIA support for full customization and side RGB lighting, it delivers both function and flair, making it ideal for students, professionals, and new mechanical keyboard users seeking value without compromise.

  • Budget-friendly Price: Ducky Ok-M delivers a premium mechanical typing experience at a budget-friendly price.
  • Great Typing Experience: Its gasket-mount design and five-layer acoustic dampening reduce pinging and echoes for a quieter, more comfortable feel.
  • Hot-Swappable Switches: Easily replace switches without soldering for effortless customization and maintenance.
  • Versatile Connectivity: Supports USB-C, BT, and 2.4GHz wireless for flexible, multi-device use.
  • VIA Support: Unlock full key and macro customization through VIA (web-based) software.
  • Side RGB: Subtle side lighting enhances the keyboard’s look with a touch of flair.

One X

The Ducky One X is the world’s first inductive keyboard, featuring Ducky inductive switches, tri-mode connection, rapid trigger, gasket-mount and seven-layer Ducky QUACK Mechanics+ dampening, high-purity PBT double shot keycaps and web-based software for customization.

One 3

The One 3 is hot-swappable and features Kailh’s hot-swap sockets, allowing users to customize their keyboards and explore the wonders of different switch types. Ducky is ecstatic for everyone to experience the incomparable typing experience from the moment they touch the keyboard!
